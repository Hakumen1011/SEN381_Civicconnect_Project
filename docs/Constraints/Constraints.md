# Constraint interactions/trade-offs

## Scope and Schedule:
Because our Schedule is fixed commitment to more functionality increases scope while still being constrained by deadlines.
This could lead to reducing scope, quality, testing efforts, increase workload and risk missing the deadline or giving an incomplete project.

## Team size and Schedule:
We are restricted to 3 teammembers and fixed deadlines, we have limited time and limited resources.
This forces us to work carefully without unncessary scope increase and lead to a rush to meet deadlines and redcuse quality to stay on schedule.
